A series in 9 lessons

Split the Octavemusic theory from square one

Never had a music lesson? Perfect. Nine short lessons that start at what is a sound and end with chords and the strange maths of the octave. Every idea is something you can hear and play, so turn your volume on.

Then drag across the strings behind this text. On an iPhone, flip off the silent switch.

Go in order: each lesson only uses words from the ones before it. Each takes about ten minutes and ends with a little ear test. Lessons you finish get a tick (it's saved in this browser).
