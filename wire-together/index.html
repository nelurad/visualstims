Wire TogetherNo. 05 · memory consolidation

Each light is a place cell in the hippocampus. It fires when you are in its spot. Move around and cells that fire together wire together, leaving a glowing thread of your path. Then sleep: the hippocampus replays that path in fast forward, over and over, and each replay copies it out to the cortex, the outer ring, where it settles into a lasting shape.
