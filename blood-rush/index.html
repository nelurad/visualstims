Blood rushNo. 04 · the BOLD signal

fMRI can't see neurons. It sees blood. When an area gets busy, nearby arteries widen and more oxygen-rich blood floods in than the neurons can use. Used-up, oxygen-poor blood is slightly magnetic and dims the MRI signal, so when the fresh blood washes it out, the spot gets brighter. That brightening is the BOLD signal.
