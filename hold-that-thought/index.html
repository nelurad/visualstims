Hold That ThoughtNo. 06 · prefrontal cortex

Your prefrontal cortex keeps a thought alive by passing it around a loop of neurons, again and again, long after you've stopped looking at it. Meanwhile the rest of your head keeps pitching distractions. Press and hold to focus: the loop tightens and the PFC crosses them out. Let go and see how long the thought survives on its own.
