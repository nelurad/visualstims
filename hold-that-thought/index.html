Hold That ThoughtNo. 06 · prefrontal cortex

A cue flashes and disappears, yet a ring of prefrontal neurons keeps firing for that spot: a bump of activity that holds the memory through the delay. Signals from the rest of the brain keep arriving. The PFC gates most of them out, but noise and the ones that slip through slowly push the bump off target. Click the ring to give it something to hold; press and hold anywhere else to focus.
